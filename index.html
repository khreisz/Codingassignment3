Key Lime Pie

Recipe by: Sally McKenney, sallysbakingaddiction.com

Ingredients

  • 11 full-sheet graham crackers (about 160g)
  • 1/2 cup salted macadamia nuts (about 62g, roasted)
  • 2 Tablespoons granulated sugar (about 25g)
  • 5 Tablespoons unsalted butter, melted (about 71g)
  • 28 ounces full-fat sweetened condensed milk (two 14-ounce cans, about 793g)
  • 1 cup key lime juice (about 240ml)
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 teaspoon key lime zest
  • For garnish: lime zest, quartered lime slices, macadamia nuts, whipped cream, or meringue topping

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C).
  2. In a food processor, pulse the graham crackers and macadamia nuts until finely ground.
  3. Add the sugar, then drizzle in the melted butter and pulse until the mixture resembles wet sand.
  4. Press the crust mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie dish.
  5. Bake the crust for 10 minutes, then remove and set aside to cool slightly.
  6. In a large bowl, whisk the egg yolks and key lime zest together until pale and creamy, about 2 minutes.
  7. Add the sweetened condensed milk and whisk until fully combined and smooth.
  8. Add the key lime juice and whisk gently just until incorporated. Do not overmix.
  9. Pour the filling into the warm crust and bake for 15–17 minutes, until the center is set with a slight jiggle.
  10. Cool at room temperature for 1 hour, then refrigerate for at least 3 hours before serving.
  11. Top with whipped cream, lime zest, lime slices, and macadamia nuts as desired. Serve chilled.
